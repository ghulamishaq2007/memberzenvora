Privacy Policy

How Zenvora Shoop collects, uses, protects, and respects your personal and order information.

Privacy Policy

At ZENVORA SHOOP, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. We believe in transparent, secure, and respectful customer relationships. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding how we gather, utilize, store, and safeguard your details when you explore our catalog, browse Pakistani lawn and gents collections, place orders via WhatsApp or our shopping cart, or interact with our customer care representatives.

By using our website, ordering our products, or communicating with us, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

To provide accurate order delivery, verified customer support, and seamless WhatsApp shopping across Karachi and Pakistan, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Customer Name: To address you respectfully and ensure proper recipient identification on courier parcels.
  • Phone / WhatsApp Number: For order confirmations, delivery address verification, courier dispatch updates, and direct WhatsApp communication.
  • Email Address: If provided during order placement, VIP membership inquiries, or general communications.
  • Delivery Address & Sector: Complete residential or commercial shipping address including Karachi area/town or Pakistan city.
  • City / Area: For accurate shipping calculations and dispatch routing.
  • Order Details: Items selected, sizes, quantities, colorways, prices, order identifiers, and order status histories.
  • Payment-Related Information: For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, payment confirmation and recipient verification; where applicable, agreed transaction references.
  • Website Usage Information: Technical data including device type, browser specifications, pages viewed, and approximate session time to ensure smooth site performance.

How We Use Your Information

We process customer information solely for legitimate retail, delivery, and customer relationship purposes:

  • Processing Orders: Preparing, packaging, and allocating stock for your requested items.
  • Delivery & Courier Dispatch: Coordinating with reliable courier personnel for timely doorstep delivery across Karachi.
  • Customer Support: Answering questions about fabric measurements, unstitched suit lengths, shoe sizes, or delivery timelines.
  • Order Confirmation: Sending prompt confirmation messages before dispatching your parcel.
  • Order Tracking: Allowing you to track your live order progress using our dedicated tracking portal.
  • Improving Products & Services: Evaluating customer feedback and trending styles to enhance our fabric selection and online experience.
  • Communicating About Orders: Notifying you in case of stock updates, delivery re-attempts, or urgent updates via WhatsApp or phone call.
  • Preventing Fraudulent Activity: Ensuring authenticity of orders, protecting genuine buyers, and preventing unauthorized or bogus orders.

Information Protection

Zenvora Shoop takes reasonable and appropriate physical, technical, and administrative measures to protect customer information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Your information is stored securely and accessed only by authorized personnel strictly on a need-to-know basis for fulfilling orders and providing customer assistance. We never sell, rent, trade, or auction customer personal details to outside commercial brokers or spam marketing agencies.

Third-Party Services

To deliver an optimal shopping experience, Zenvora Shoop collaborates with select trusted third-party service providers. These third-party services process limited information only to the extent necessary to perform their specific functions on our behalf:

  • Courier & Delivery Partners: Sharing recipient name, delivery address, and contact number to complete doorstep parcel handoff.
  • Hosting & Database Infrastructure (Firebase): Secure cloud infrastructure to record order identifiers, sync real-time inventory, and verify membership statuses.
  • Analytics & Site Performance: Anonymized site metrics to monitor website speed, uptime, and accessibility.
  • Payment & Communication Channels: Authorized channels such as WhatsApp and banking gateways where applicable for processing agreed payments.

Cookies

The Zenvora Shoop website may use cookies, local storage, or similar browser technologies to optimize site performance and enrich your user experience. These technical cookies allow us to:

  • Remember items added to your shopping cart as you navigate between pages.
  • Retain temporary preferences and session continuity.
  • Ensure fast page loading times across modern mobile and desktop browsers.

You can modify your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer; however, disabling certain cookies may affect shopping cart storage and interactive features.

Customer Rights

We respect your rights regarding your personal information. As a valued customer, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of the personal data we hold about your past orders.
  • Request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete contact or delivery information.
  • Request that we cease contacting you for promotional updates.
  • Contact us directly with any questions, comments, or concerns regarding your privacy.

Policy Updates

Zenvora Shoop reserves the right to revise or update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect improvements in our operations, updated service features, or applicable legal requirements.

Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage customers to periodically review this page to stay informed about how we safeguard their data.
