Return & Refund Policy

Clear, straightforward terms for product inspection, eligible returns, exchanges, and refund processing.

Return & Refund Policy

At ZENVORA SHOOP, we take genuine pride in delivering exquisite Pakistani fabrics, beautifully tailored ladies and gents suits, and handcrafted footwear. Our primary goal is for every customer to receive products in completely satisfactory, pristine condition.

We inspect each parcel before packaging to ensure high quality standards. If, however, you encounter an unexpected issue with your order, this policy explains how returns, exchanges, and refunds are handled fairly and transparently.

Return Eligibility

A product may be eligible for a return or replacement under any of the following verified conditions:

  • Wrong Product Received: If the item, design, colorway, or product model delivered does not match what you ordered.
  • Damaged Product Received: If the merchandise arrived physically damaged, torn, or broken during courier transit.
  • Manufacturing Defect: If there is a verifiable flaw in the fabric weaving, printing anomaly, severe discoloration, or structural footwear defect.
  • Significantly Different from Description: If the specifications or essential features of the item differ substantially from the catalog listing.

Important Notice: The customer should contact ZENVORA SHOOP as soon as possible after receiving the order so our support team can immediately initiate verification and resolve the matter without delay.

Non-Returnable Situations

To uphold rigorous hygiene and authentic quality standards, returns and refunds cannot be approved in the following reasonable circumstances:

  • Customer Misuse or Accidental Damage: Items damaged due to rough handling, improper laundering, snagging, or excessive strain after delivery.
  • Used, Washed, or Altered Items: Any garment that has been worn, tailored, cut, stitched, chemically cleaned, washed, or altered from its original dispatch state. Footwear showing outdoor sole wear or creased uppers cannot be accepted.
  • Missing Original Tags & Packaging: Products returned without their original brand tags, accessories, dust bags, or essential packaging.
  • Change of Mind: Situations where an order was accurately fulfilled according to catalog specifications but the customer simply had a change of preference.
  • Specially Marked Final Sale / Clearance Items: Products expressly sold as non-returnable or clearance clearance deals.

Return Time Period

Customers must submit a return or exchange request within 7 days after delivery (from the timestamp the courier parcel was handed over at your doorstep).

Requests submitted after this 7-day window cannot be accepted for return processing. We strongly urge customers to inspect their package promptly upon arrival.

How To Request A Return

To initiate an authorized return or replacement, please follow this straightforward process:

  1. Contact ZENVORA SHOOP: Reach out immediately via our official WhatsApp at 03232974451 or through our Contact page.
  2. Provide Order Details: Share your Order ID (e.g. ZV-2026-XXXX), customer full name, and the delivery date.
  3. Explain the Reason: Describe clearly why you are requesting a return or exchange (such as incorrect size, wrong item, or transit damage).
  4. Provide Photos or Videos: Share clear photos or a short video showcasing the product tags, defect, or incorrect item received as requested by customer care.
  5. Wait for Return Approval: Our team will review the claim and issue an authorization confirmation along with return reference instructions.
  6. Follow Return Instructions: Securely package the item in its original box/packing and hand it over according to the dispatch instructions provided by ZENVORA SHOOP.

Refunds

Once the returned product is received at our Karachi dispatch hub, our quality control team will inspect the item to verify that it meets the return criteria outlined above.

Approved refunds are processed according to the original payment method and applicable circumstances:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Because Cash on Delivery payments are collected in physical cash by third-party courier couriers, refunds cannot be handed back in physical cash at your doorstep. Instead, refund arrangements are coordinated through an agreed digital transfer method with the customer (such as Pakistani Bank Transfer, EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or store voucher).
  • Processing Timeline: In order to allow thorough inspection and verification, refunds are typically processed within 3 to 5 business days following our receipt and approval of the returned item. We do not provide instant or on-the-spot refunds prior to product inspection.

Exchange

If you wish to exchange an item for a different footwear size or an alternative design, exchanges are subject to current product availability in our live inventory.

If the requested replacement item or size is out of stock, you may choose an alternative product of equivalent value or receive an agreed refund in accordance with our refund policy.

Delivery Charges

Return and replacement delivery charges depend on the underlying reason for the return:

  • Store Error or Defect: If the return or exchange is due to a mistake by ZENVORA SHOOP (such as delivering a damaged item, defective fabric, or wrong color/article), ZENVORA SHOOP will cover the return courier shipping costs.
  • Customer-Requested Preference or Size Swap: If an exchange is requested by the customer where the originally dispatched item was accurately supplied as ordered (e.g. voluntary shoe size swap), the customer is responsible for the standard two-way courier delivery fee.
